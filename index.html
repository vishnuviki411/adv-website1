About Me

Advocate R. Sivamanikumaran is a seasoned lawyer in Tamil Nadu, practicing across District Courts and the High Court of Madras. Enrolled with the Bar Council of Tamil Nadu in 2011, he brings over 15 years of expertise in civil litigation, property disputes, corporate law, and family matters. His reputation is built on consistent advocacy, integrity, and client‑focused solutions. Recognized by the Government of India as a Notary Public, he serves the public at large with certified documentation and legal attestation services. He is also a trusted panel advocate for leading banks, regularly handling complex civil litigation and compliance matters. As an authorized panel advocate for India Post Payment Bank, he provides specialized representation in financial and consumer disputes.

Through his firm, Apex Lawroot Legal Solutions, Advocate Sivamanikumaran has established a strong presence in Tamil Nadu’s legal landscape. For over 15 years, the firm has acted as a reliable partner for individuals, businesses, and institutions, bridging the gap between complex legal frameworks and practical, results‑driven outcomes. The practice is founded on the belief that every dispute, contract, and compliance challenge requires both precise technical insight and a strategic human approach.

Over the past decade and a half, he has specialized in high‑stakes civil and corporate disputes, while streamlining document drafting, vetting, and lifecycle management. His services extend to regulatory compliance, succession certificates, consumer disputes, divorce matters, and property law opinions. Whether guiding clients through commercial agreements, resolving civil litigation, or offering online legal opinion reports for property transactions, his focus remains clear: mitigating risk, protecting vital interests, and delivering peace of mind.
